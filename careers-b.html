Governance & Risk Advisor

Sydney, Australia (On Site)

talent@deltatango.com.au

Join Delta Tango Advisory as a Governance & Risk Advisor and support organisations operating in complex, high-trust environments. You will work across strategy, assurance, and delivery, helping clients strengthen controls, improve decision-making, and navigate risk with confidence.

Job Description

What will make your journey with us unique?

You will work closely with experienced practitioners on meaningful projects across government, defence, and regulated industries. You will be trusted to contribute from day one, with exposure to real client challenges, hands-on mentoring, and the opportunity to shape how organisations govern, manage risk, and deliver outcomes.

Requirement

What will you do?

You will support the design and implementation of governance frameworks, risk models, and assurance approaches that help organisations operate with clarity and confidence. Working across multiple engagements, you will contribute to reporting structures, stakeholder coordination, process improvement, and delivery of practical, evidence-based recommendations.

What you will bring

  • Experience working in governance, risk, compliance, or advisory environments
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to communicate clearly with technical and non-technical stakeholders
  • Confidence working across structured programs or regulated industries
  • Strong attention to detail and a practical, outcomes-focused mindset
