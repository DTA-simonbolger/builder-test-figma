Questions? Let us clarify.

Quick answers to the questions leaders ask most before partnering with Delta Tango, covering capability, approach and expectations.

What does Delta Tango Advisory do?

We assess, assure, design, and deliver capability across strategy, operating models, governance, and program delivery. We help leaders make decisions and deliver work that stands up to scrutiny.

Who do you work with and what challenges do you solve?

We work with Defence, national security, regulated corporates, and critical programs where governance, delivery confidence, and auditability matter.

How are you different from larger consulting firms?

We bring small senior teams, practical delivery experience, and evidence-led advice focused on outcomes, not large engagement overhead.

Both. We can advise, assure, design the operating model, and work directly with teams to deliver the agreed outcomes.

What does an engagement look like and what outcomes can we expect?

We define the problem, agree the decision points, establish practical controls, and leave your team with clear artefacts, governance rhythms, and accountable next steps.
